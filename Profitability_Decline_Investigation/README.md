
# Profitability Decline Investigation

## Objective
Investigate changes in business profitability by analyzing revenue,
cost of goods sold, sales volume, discounts, and product-level performance.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib

## Key Analysis
- Monthly revenue and gross profit trends
- Gross profit margin changes
- First-six-month versus last-six-month comparison
- Product-level profitability analysis
- Sales volume and discount analysis
- Potential cost-related profitability issues

## Methodology
1. Generate synthetic product sales data.
2. Calculate revenue, costs, gross profit, and margins.
3. Compare business performance across two periods.
4. Identify products with declining gross profit.
5. Visualize profitability trends and discount changes.
6. Develop management recommendations.
7. Export detailed results to CSV files.

## Business Value
This analysis demonstrates how businesses can investigate
profitability changes, identify potential problem areas, and
prioritize further financial investigation.

## Dataset Disclaimer
The data is synthetic and generated for educational purposes.
The findings do not represent actual company performance.
The analysis examines potential drivers and does not establish
causation.
