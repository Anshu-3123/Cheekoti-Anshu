
# What-If Business Simulator

## Objective
Simulate business scenarios and evaluate how changes in sales volume,
selling price, variable costs, and fixed costs affect profitability.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib

## Key Features
- Baseline business performance calculation
- Revenue and total cost estimation
- Profit and profit margin calculation
- Break-even sales volume estimation
- Scenario comparison
- Best- and worst-case scenario identification
- Custom business assumptions
- CSV export of scenario results

## Methodology
1. Define baseline business assumptions.
2. Calculate financial performance indicators.
3. Model alternative business scenarios.
4. Compare profit, revenue, costs, and margins.
5. Visualize scenario results.
6. Generate management recommendations.
7. Export results for further analysis.

## Business Value
This simulator demonstrates how businesses can evaluate possible
financial outcomes before making pricing, sales, or cost decisions.

## Dataset Disclaimer
The project uses illustrative business assumptions rather than
real company data. Results are estimates and should not be treated
as guaranteed financial outcomes.
