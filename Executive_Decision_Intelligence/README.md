
# Executive Decision Intelligence

## Objective
Develop an executive dashboard that consolidates business performance indicators to support management decisions.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib

## Key Performance Indicators
- Total revenue
- Total cost
- Total profit
- Profit margin
- Sales target achievement
- Customer satisfaction
- On-time delivery performance
- Revenue per employee

## Methodology
1. Generate sample business-unit data.
2. Calculate key financial and operational KPIs.
3. Classify business-unit performance.
4. Visualize revenue, profit margins, and sales target achievement.
5. Identify areas requiring management attention.
6. Generate business recommendations.
7. Export detailed results to CSV.

## Business Value
This project demonstrates how executives can use consolidated
business indicators to identify performance gaps, investigate
operational issues, and prioritize business improvement initiatives.

## Dataset Disclaimer
The dataset is synthetic and created for educational purposes.
The results do not represent the performance of a real company.
Recommendations are illustrative and should be validated using
actual business data before making decisions.
