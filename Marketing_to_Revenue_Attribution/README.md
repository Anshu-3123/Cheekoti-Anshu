
# Marketing-to-Revenue Attribution

## Objective
Analyze the customer journey from campaign exposure to revenue and identify funnel drop-offs.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib

## Methodology
1. Generate sample campaign data.
2. Measure clicks, leads, conversions, and revenue.
3. Calculate funnel conversion rates.
4. Compare campaign performance.
5. Identify customer drop-off stages.
6. Recommend marketing improvements.

## Dataset
Synthetic data generated for educational purposes.

## Disclaimer
The results are illustrative and do not establish causal marketing impact.
